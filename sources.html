Location and weather / الموقع والطقس

Open-Meteo / GeoNames: submitted city searches only, no autocomplete. Not parcel search.

Weather data by Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0. Best-match numerical weather models; model-dependent resolution, current 15-minute timestep, local timezone shown. Not station measurements. Units are checked before display; null never becomes zero. Sampling uses the arithmetic mean of polygon vertices, not a field average. Requests time out after 25 seconds with no automatic retry.

Free Open-Meteo endpoint: non-commercial use, limits of 10,000 calls/day, 5,000/hour, 600/minute. Explicit actions only. No availability guarantee.

Climate / المناخ

World Bank CCKP: CMIP6 0.25-degree multi-model median annual temperature anomaly relative to 1995–2014. Provider's country-aggregated value; no field extrapolation. Country is an explicit user input independent of map search. SSP1–2.6, SSP2–4.5, SSP5–8.5 and four 20-year horizons change the actual query. No invented warming coefficients or crop impact multipliers.

Metadata · Dataset and licensing. The app currently uses the legacy v1 public endpoint because it allows browser requests in this local prototype. In QA, the documented v3 data-catalog endpoint did not allow cross-origin reads from the app origin; if v1 is retired, a same-origin proxy or CORS-enabled official source is required. Missing data never falls back to another model or geography. A single median does not describe ensemble uncertainty. Conditional projections, not forecasts.

Geometry and crop comparison

Spherical longitude/sine-latitude area formula, mean Earth radius 6,371,008.8 m. Divide square metres by 10,000 for hectares; multiply hectares by 2.47105381 for US acres. Self-intersections, duplicate vertices, degenerate shapes, latitude beyond ±85°, over 100 corners and extents over 2° are refused. No antimeridian support. Not a cadastral survey.

Rotation descriptors count unique botanical families, legume seasons and adjacent same-family transitions. No quantified yield, nitrogen credit, suitability, water demand or return. FAO crop diversification context.

NASA POWER & Water Truth / بيانات NASA POWER وحقيقة المياه

NASA POWER Daily Point API is queried at the coordinate average of the farmer-drawn field vertices for the previous completed calendar year by default. The application requests PRECTOTCORR (daily corrected precipitation), T2M_MAX and T2M_MIN (daily 2 m temperature extrema), and ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN (daily downward shortwave radiation). POWER daily point values are gridded historical climate estimates; each parameter retains its original source spatial resolution. The requested point is a spatial sample, not a farm sensor or field-wide measurement. Daily requests default to Local Solar Time (LST); the selected period, coordinates, units and source remain visible. The solar-radiation series is displayed as context but is not an input to the current temperature-only ETo equation.

FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 (FAO-56) is the methodological reference. When wind and humidity data are absent, reference evapotranspiration (ETo) uses the FAO-56 Hargreaves–Samani temperature-only approximation: ETo = 0.0023 × (Tmean + 17.8) × √(Tmax − Tmin) × Ra. Tmean is in °C and extraterrestrial radiation Ra is converted to mm/day, so ETo is mm/day. This is not FAO-56 Penman–Monteith. The current NASA POWER request does not include the wind and humidity inputs needed for the preferred Penman–Monteith method; the app does not infer those missing values.

Crop evapotranspiration is estimated as ETc = Kc × ETo, using opt-in, illustrative FAO-56 crop-coefficient ranges and growth-stage durations. These reference rows span named regions and seasons; they are not locally validated for the farmer's cultivar, soil, planting calendar or field. The farmer supplies the rotation, planting dates, field boundary and irrigation budget. The budget is treated as field-applied irrigation in mm or m³ for each season or the whole plan, not source withdrawal.

Estimated irrigation need = max(0, ETc − Pe). Effective precipitation Pe follows the simple FAO rainfall equations per complete 30-day accounting block: max(0, 0.6P − 10) for P < 75 mm; otherwise max(0, 0.8P − 25), where P is precipitation in that block. FAO presents these as monthly estimates and limits the simple method to fields with roughly 4–5% maximum slope; Field Shift's consecutive 30-day bins are an explicit approximation and require farmer confirmation of slope applicability. The final incomplete block receives no rainfall credit. The seasonal output is a historical-climate scenario, not a forecast; missing observations or unsupported crop settings remain UNKNOWN.

This prototype does not measure actual water savings, basin-wide consumptive use, groundwater availability, soil-water storage, irrigation efficiency, delivery losses or future rainfall. It does not estimate yields or profits. Local crop calendars, coefficients, soil/root-zone data, irrigation system performance and agronomist review are needed before operational use.

تستخدم وحدة حقيقة المياه بيانات NASA POWER اليومية التاريخية عند متوسط إحداثيات زوايا الحقل، وليست قياساً من حساس داخل المزرعة. يُقدّر ETo بمعادلة Hargreaves–Samani عند نقص الرياح والرطوبة، وليس بمعادلة Penman–Monteith. إعدادات Kc ومراحل النمو توضيحية وغير معتمدة محلياً. ميزانية المياه يقدّمها المزارع بوصفها كمية مطبّقة على الحقل؛ والنتيجة سيناريو تاريخي لا توقّع. لا تقيس الوحدة التوفير الفعلي أو المياه الجوفية أو استهلاك الحوض، وتبقى النتيجة UNKNOWN عند نقص مدخلات أساسية.

Plans use localStorage or portable JSON. No account, analytics, server storage or paid API. MapLibre GL JS 5.3.0 (BSD-3-Clause) JS/CSS loads from unpkg; no external fonts. Internet and WebGL are required for maps. Coordinate editing and rotation counts work without them.
